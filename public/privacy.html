Tradies Toolbox

Privacy policy

Effective 16 September 2026 · Seller Joshua Pearson (Apple Individual) · Bundle id com.chippystoolbox.app

Tradies Toolbox is published by Joshua Pearson as an Apple Individual (not a company account). It is an on-site calculator app for Australian trades (concrete volume, stair set-out, running measurements, and a right-angle triangle). It is offline-first, has no ads, and does not create accounts.

This page is the public policy for the App Store and Google Play. Plain language: we do not collect a profile of you, and we do not sell data.

What we collect

Essentially nothing beyond what Apple or Google already handle for a store purchase.

  • Measurements and calculations stay on your device. Lengths, volumes, stair figures, and speech read-outs are not sent to our servers. We do not run an account database.
  • No login, analytics, advertising, or crash telemetry are bundled in this app.
  • Optional unlock ($9.99 AUD, one-time) for stair set-out, concrete volume, and set-out is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play. They may keep purchase receipts under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your card number, and we do not get a copy of your full payment details.
  • A small on-device flag may remember that you unlocked, so the paid tools stay available offline. That flag does not leave the device.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information.
  • We do not show ads or share data with ad networks.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not require an email address, name, or location permission to use the calculators.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled by Apple or Google, not by us. To review, restore, or refund a purchase, use your Apple ID or Google account settings, or those companies’ support. Their rules apply to receipts and payment data.

Australian users

The app is built for tradies in Australia. If we ever held personal information covered by the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), we would handle it in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Today the calculators themselves do not need that information to work.

Children

Tradies Toolbox is a trade reference tool, not a children’s app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes in a material way (for example if a future build adds optional diagnostics), we will update this page before that release ships.
